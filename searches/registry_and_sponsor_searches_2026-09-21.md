# Registry and sponsor-repository searches for unpublished or incompletely reported DXA outcomes (second revision, Reviewer 3, point 10)

Date of searches: 21 September 2026 (BA; queries run through the public search interfaces / public API of each register). Scope: phase 3 randomized trials of GLP-1 receptor agonists or dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists (plus newer GLP-1-based agents, screened for completeness) with DXA-measured body composition as a registered or reported outcome. Purpose: identify trials with DXA outcomes that are unpublished, posted only in a register or in a sponsor document, or incompletely reported.

## 3. Clinical Trials Information System (CTIS; trials authorised under Regulation 536/2014, 2023 onward)

Queries (public search API, "contain all"): `absorptiometry` (22 records), `DXA` (53), `DEXA` (33), `lean body mass` (13), `fat-free mass` (9), `body composition` (61); records whose title, short title or endpoint text named a GLP-1-based agent were retained: 24 trials, of which 16 phase 3 or phase 4 and 8 phase 2. ## 4. Sponsor trial-document repositories

### 4a. Novo Nordisk (novonordisk-trials.com, "Find Study Results")

Searched 21 September 2026. The repository lists 914 studies and provides, without registration, a redacted clinical trial report (CTR) synopsis for most studies, a full redacted CTR for some, and a layperson summary; the site states that full redacted study reports may be requested at dataaccess@novonordisk-trials.com (the enquiry described in section 5 was addressed to dataaccess@novonordisk.com and was answered).

Free-text queries for `DXA`, `DEXA`, `absorptiometry` and `body composition` returned no results, whereas `liraglutide` returned 103 studies and `semaglutide` 92. The search field therefore indexes study identifiers, titles and drug names, not document contents, and cannot be used to find DXA substudies directly.

Screening at the level of the CTR *synopsis* is also insensitive, which we verified against the two trials in this review whose DXA data come from a Novo Nordisk CTR. In the full redacted CTRs of LEAD-2 (NN2211-1572) and LEAD-3 (NN2211-1573), the first mention of DXA occurs on PDF pages 31 and 32 respectively, that is, after the synopsis section; the synopsis itself lists only body weight, body mass index and waist and hip circumference among the anthropometric outcomes, and does not mention the DXA substudy at all. The DXA results themselves are in Tables 11-51 and 11-52 (LEAD-2) and Tables 11-44 and 11-45 (LEAD-3), several hundred pages into the reports. A search restricted to synopses would therefore have missed both trials.

Consistent with this, screening of the 25 publicly posted liraglutide CTR synopses for trials in type 2 diabetes or obesity of at least 16 weeks (LEAD-1, LEAD-4, LEAD-5, LEAD-6, LEADER, SCALE Sleep Apnoea, SCALE Insulin, SCALE IBT, LIRA-PRIME, LIRA-SWITCH, LIRA-ADD2SGLT2i and the Japanese and Chinese programme trials) found no mention of DXA, DEXA, absorptiometry, body composition, lean mass, fat mass or fat-free mass in any of them; anthropometric outcomes were limited throughout to body weight, body mass index and waist, hip or neck circumference. The synopses of SCALE Obesity and Prediabetes (NN8022-1839), SCALE Diabetes (NN8022-1922) and the weight-maintenance trial NN8022-1923 likewise report only body weight, body mass index, waist circumference and excess body weight. Given the demonstrated insensitivity of synopsis screening, these negative results cannot exclude unreported DXA substudies in these trials; they only establish that no DXA outcome is reported in the publicly posted synopses.

### 4b. Eli Lilly (tirzepatide)

Lilly does not operate an equivalent open repository of redacted clinical trial reports. Lilly registers its interventional studies on ClinicalTrials.gov (searched, section 1) and shares clinical study reports and participant-level data through the Vivli platform on request, with a separate enquiry route for study documents without participant-level data (checked 21 September 2026). Requests are reviewed by Vivli, by the sponsor and by an independent review panel, and access requires a research proposal; documents are not downloadable without that process. The DXA substudy of SURMOUNT-1 is published in full (Look et al. 2025), so no unreported DXA outcome was sought for tirzepatide beyond the registry search.

### 4c. Other sponsors

AstraZeneca (exenatide), Sanofi (lixisenatide), Boehringer Ingelheim (survodutide) and GSK (albiglutide) likewise share clinical study reports through Vivli on request rather than by open download. No completed phase 3 trial of these agents with an unreported DXA outcome in an eligible population was identified in the registry searches (section 1), so no document request was made.

## 5. Correspondence with the sponsor

This contact predates the original submission and is documented in the corresponding author's e-mail record.

**Enquiry sent 27 February 2026**, from BA to Novo Nordisk clinical trial data access (dataaccess@novonordisk.com). Subject: "Inquiry Regarding Summary Statistics for LEAD-2 and LEAD-3 DXA Substudies (Lean Body Mass Variance Data)". Content: the enquiry stated that the DXA substudies of LEAD-2 (NCT00318461) and LEAD-3 (NCT00294723) were to be included in a systematic review and meta-analysis of DXA-measured lean body mass; that the body-composition outcomes published by Jendle et al. (Diabetes Obes Metab 2009; doi 10.1111/j.1463-1326.2009.01158.x) present mean changes as bar charts without explicit variance measures; and that aggregate summary statistics (mean and standard error or standard deviation) for the absolute change in lean or fat-free mass in each arm were therefore requested. The enquiry stated explicitly that individual participant data were not being requested, and asked whether aggregate summary data could be shared directly or whether a formal request through the Vivli platform was required.

**Reply received 10 March 2026**, from Novo Nordisk A/S Clinical Disclosure (Cross Portfolio Excellence, Transparency and Archive), reference CSR 2026.002. The reply directed the enquirer to the study reports available for download at novonordisk-trials.com under the identifiers NN2211-1572 (LEAD-2) and NN2211-1573 (LEAD-3), and included screenshots of the relevant repository entries.

**Consequence for this review.** The redacted clinical trial reports of LEAD-2 and LEAD-3 were downloaded from that repository following this reply, and the lean-mass and fat-mass values entered into the meta-analysis for both trials were extracted from those reports (LEAD-2, Tables 11-51 and 11-52; LEAD-3, Tables 11-44 and 11-45), not from the bar charts of the 2009 publication. The sponsor did not provide any data beyond what is in the public reports, and did not indicate that further unreported DXA data exist.

**Not done.** No further enquiry was sent to Novo Nordisk about DXA substudies in other trials (for example the SCALE programme), and no enquiry was sent to the corresponding authors of the four included DXA reports or to Eli Lilly. Drafts for these, if BA decides to send them, are in 18_sponsor_author_emails.md. Note that the February 2026 enquiry was specific to LEAD-2 and LEAD-3 variance data, so it does not by itself establish that we asked the sponsor about unreported DXA substudies in general; the text must not overstate this.

**Separate from the above:** BA registered on the Vivli platform in February 2026 and a "Vivli Academic Data Contributor Agreement" was issued and later voided. This concerns depositing the review team's own data, not a data request to a sponsor, and is therefore not reported as a search effort. (BA to confirm.)
